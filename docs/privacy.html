Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What the extension reads

To show you your Chrome profiles, Profile Manager reads, on your computer only: each profile's name, the email of the Google account signed in to it, its picture, when it was last used, and how much disk space its folder takes. This comes from Chrome's own files on your computer.

What leaves your computer

None of that information leaves your computer. It isn't uploaded, stored on a server, sold or shared with anyone. The helper app has no network access features and runs only when you use the extension.

For the subscription, payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. When you start a subscription you enter your email and card details on their secure pages. We never see your card number. The extension asks ExtensionPay whether your subscription is active.

What is stored

The extension keeps a random identifier and your subscription status in Chrome's extension storage on your computer. The helper keeps a small log of what it deleted, on your computer only.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the extension removes its storage. Uninstalling the helper (Windows Settings > Apps) removes the helper and its log. To cancel a subscription, use "Manage subscription" in the extension.
