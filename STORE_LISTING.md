## Detailed description

Delete multiple Chrome profiles at once, instead of removing them one by one.

Chrome only lets you delete one profile at a time, and you have to open each one to do it. Profile Manager shows every Chrome profile on your computer in a single list. Tick the ones you don't need, click Delete, and they're all gone.

Requires a small free helper app (Windows and Mac). The extension walks you through the one-minute setup the first time you open it.

★ WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See all your Chrome profiles in one place, with name, Google account email, picture, last used date and disk space
• Select many profiles and delete them in one click
• Sort by "last used" to find old profiles, or by size to free up the most disk space
• Search profiles by name or email
• Your tabs and windows come back automatically after the cleanup

★ SAFE BY DESIGN
• The profile you're using can never be deleted
• You see exactly what will be removed and confirm before anything happens
• Chrome's profile list is backed up before every change
• Anything synced to your Google account stays in your account

★ HOW IT WORKS
Chrome doesn't allow extensions to delete files on your computer, so Profile Manager uses a small free helper app (Windows and Mac). The first time you open the extension, it walks you through a one-minute setup: download the helper, open it, click OK. The helper runs only on your computer and never uploads anything.

★ PRICING
Delete up to 3 profiles free. Unlock unlimited bulk deleting with a one-time $4.99 payment. No subscription.

★ GREAT FOR
• People who created profiles for old jobs, clients, schools or test accounts
• Freelancers and agencies managing many client accounts
• IT staff cleaning up shared or family computers
• Anyone whose Chrome profile picker has gotten out of hand

## Getting found and ranked (after launch)
1. **Ratings matter most.** The extension asks for a review once, right after someone's first successful cleanup (when they're happiest). For this to work, put the store link in `STORE_URL` in `extension/config.js`.
2. **Get the first 10–20 installs and reviews fast.** Ask friends and colleagues, and post where people complain about this problem: Reddit r/chrome and r/techsupport, the Google Chrome Help Community, and replies to "how to delete multiple Chrome profiles" questions.
3. **The landing page targets Google search** for "how to delete multiple chrome profiles at once". It has an FAQ marked up so Google can show it directly in results. Add the store link in `docs/index.html` (`STORE_URL`) once you have it.
4. **Keep the name and short description stable.** Changing them often resets what the store has learned about your listing.
5. **Reply to every review**, especially negative ones. The store and future users see it.
